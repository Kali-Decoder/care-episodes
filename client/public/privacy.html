Privacy Policy

Care Episode Agent · Last updated: August 2026

Care Episode Agent is a hackathon project (Google “All Things Agentic” Hackathon). It is a demonstration application and is not medical advice. The agent summarises and flags information for a single demo patient; it does not diagnose, and a licensed doctor should review all results.

What this app does

Care Episode Agent lets a patient upload a doctor’s prescription and lab reports. It extracts the ordered tests and reported values, finds nearby diagnostic labs, sends booking requests, and compares lab results against prior readings to flag meaningful changes.

Information we process

  • Uploaded documents (prescriptions, lab reports) — processed to extract clinical information such as ordered tests, reported values, and reference ranges.
  • Extracted clinical data — stored to track the care episode over time and compare results against history.
  • This demo operates on a single, non-identifiable demo patient. Please do not upload real personal or third-party medical information.

Google API services

With your authorisation, the app uses Google APIs to send a booking-request email (Gmail API, gmail.send) and to create a tentative calendar hold (Google Calendar API, calendar.events). These actions are performed only as part of the care-episode workflow. The app’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it except as needed to provide the app’s features.

Data processing & storage

Extracted clinical data is stored in Google Cloud (Firestore) to support the episode timeline and trend comparison. Document extraction uses Google’s Gemini models via Vertex AI. Data is used solely to operate the app’s features and is not sold or used for advertising.

Data retention & deletion

As a demonstration project, data may be removed at any time. To request deletion of any data associated with the demo, contact us at the email below.
